# Gmail — usage notes for the planner

- Inbox: emails in a list; tap one to read it.
- Search: tap the search bar at the top, type the sender or subject.
- Compose: tap the Compose (pencil) button, fill To / Subject / body, tap Send.
- To find a specific email, search for it — do not scroll the inbox hoping it appears.
- Do not send email unless the goal explicitly asks for it.
