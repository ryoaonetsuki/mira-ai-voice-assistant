# YouTube — usage notes for the planner

- Search icon: located in the top bar, magnifier icon (may be icon-only or labeled Search); use vision coordinates when the node tree has no match.
- Screen after tapping search: search field appears at the top, and the soft keyboard opens.
- Typing in search: typing needs the field focused first (tap field, wait for keyboard to appear, set text via ACTION_SET_TEXT, read back to verify, then press Enter); tap a result to play it.
- Home feed: videos in a vertical list; feed and video list scrolls are normal and expected; repeated scrolls are not a loop failure.
- Playing: tap the video once to show controls (pause/play, seek bar); swipe down on the player to minimise it.
- Subscriptions / Library: bottom navigation bar.
- To play a specific song or video, search for it by name — do not scroll the home feed hoping it appears.
