# TikTok — usage notes for the planner

- For You feed: one full-screen video at a time; swipe up for the next video, swipe down for the previous.
- Search: tap the magnifier at the top right, type the query, press Enter; tap a result to watch it.
- A video plays automatically when it appears; tap once to pause, tap again to resume.
- Like: double-tap the video. Follow: tap the + on the creator's avatar.
- To watch a specific creator or topic, search for it — do not swipe the feed hoping it appears.
