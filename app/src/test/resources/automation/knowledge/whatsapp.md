# WhatsApp — usage notes for the planner

- Chats list: tap a chat to open it; type in the message box at the bottom and tap Send.
- Search: tap the magnifier at the top, type the contact or message text.
- New chat: tap the new-chat button (bottom right), pick a contact.
- To message a specific person, search for their name in the Chats list — do not scroll the list hoping to find them.
- Calls tab: for voice/video calls; do not place a call unless the goal asks for one.
